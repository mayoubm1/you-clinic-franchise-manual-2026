2.1 Medical Governance Framework & Clinical Accountability

To protect the medical integrity of You Wellness Clinic across all franchised territories, every operational unit must strictly adhere to a bifurcated governance structure that severs clinical decision-making from commercial objectives.

CLINICAL GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTABILITY [Global Medical Advisory Board (HQ)] ├── Establishes Evidence-Based Formularies & Clinical Protocols ├── Conducts Bi-Annual Medical Quality & Compliance Audits └── Approves New Diagnostic Testing Panels & Experimental Therapies [Franchise Medical Director (Local Licensee)] ├── Retains 100% Autonomy Over Patient Prescriptions & Treatment Plans ├── Oversees Local Medical Licensing, Safety, & Escalation Protocols └── Ensures Absolute Compliance with Local Health Authority Regulations
🏛️ Global Medical Advisory Board (HQ)

Responsible for defining master clinical protocols, validating new diagnostic technologies, setting standardized dosing caps, and establishing strict boundaries for off-label therapies and nutraceutical compounding.

مجلس الإستشارات الطبي العالمي (المقر الرئيسي): مسؤول عن وضع البروتوكولات السريرية، والتحقق من التقنيات التشخيصية، وضبط الحدود الصارمة للعلاجات المتقدمة.
⚕️ Franchise Medical Director (Local/Regional)

Every licensed location must employ a fully licensed, locally credentialed Physician as Medical Director. The Medical Director holds ultimate clinical responsibility and operates under the Corporate Practice of Medicine (CPOM) framework, ensuring zero commercial interference in medical judgments.

المدير الطبي للفرع: يتولى المسؤولية الطبية الكاملة ويتمتع باستقلالية تامة وفق إطار (CPOM) لمنع أي تدخل تجاري في القرارات العلاجية.

2.2 Evidence-Based Protocol Categorization & Informed Consent

All clinical offerings within the You Wellness Clinic ecosystem are categorized into three distinct operational tiers to maintain absolute transparency, regulatory compliance, and patient safety:

Tier 1

Standard-of-Care & Preventive

Scope: Standard diagnostic blood panels, basic micronutrient IV drips (hydration, vitamin C, B-complex), continuous glucose monitoring (CGM), and lifestyle medicine.
Regulatory Basis: Fully approved by local health authorities (DHA/MOHAP in UAE, FDA/EMA standards).
Consent: Standard Medical Consent Form covering general clinical risks, venipuncture protocols, and basic data privacy.
Tier 2

Compound & Off-Label Precision

Scope: Customized IV nutrient infusions, compounding peptides (NAD+, Glutathione, targeted amino acid formulations), and specific biological age reversal protocols.
Regulatory Basis: Prepared strictly under 503A/503B compounding standards or national pharmacy compounding frameworks based on individual medical prescriptions.
Consent: Tier 2 Expanded Informed Consent, detailing compounding rationale, individual formulation benefits, side effects, and observational data.
Tier 3

Advanced Regenerative & Exosomes

Scope: Cell-derived therapies, exosome infusions, advanced stem cell derivatives, and novel longevity interventions.
Regulatory Basis: Governed by national regenerative medicine frameworks (EU-ATMP, Japan RM Act Class I-III, US FDA IND/REBS oversight, CDSCO/ICMR).
Consent: Comprehensive Bio-Ethical Consent covering cell sourcing, donor screening, sterility verification, GMP manufacturing, and explicit functional optimization goals.

2.3 Clinical Escalation, Stop Rules & Emergency Protocols

1. Pre-Treatment Screening
  • Mandatory baseline vitals (blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, ECG when indicated).
  • Comprehensive screening for organ function (renal and hepatic panels) prior to high-dose IV administration or peptide therapies.
  • Absolute contraindication checks (active oncological conditions, acute renal failure, pregnancy/lactation, severe cardiovascular instability).
2. Immediate Treatment Stop Rules

Treatment must be aborted immediately if any of the following occur:

  • Acute allergic or anaphylactoid reaction during IV administration.
  • Sudden drop or surge in blood pressure beyond safety thresholds (≥30% deviation from baseline).
  • Patient expression of acute discomfort, chest tightness, shortness of breath, or severe headache.
3. Emergency Response & Transfer
  • On-Site Crash Cart: Automated External Defibrillator (AED), airway management, epinephrine (1:1000), antihistamines, IV corticosteroids, and oxygen.
  • Transfer Agreement: Formal Transfer Agreement or explicit emergency escalation pathway with a tertiary care hospital within a 15-minute transport radius.

2.4 Pharmacovigilance, Adverse Event Reporting & Incident Workflows

⏱️ Mandatory Incident Reporting (12-Hour Window)

Any Adverse Event (AE) or Serious Adverse Event (SAE) must be logged into the central EHR portal within 12 hours of occurrence and reported directly to the Franchise Medical Director and HQ Medical Governance Board.

🔍 Root-Cause Analysis (RCA) (48-Hour Window)

An SAE triggers an immediate clinical hold on the specific batch or compound used across the clinic, followed by a formal RCA investigation completed and submitted to HQ within 48 hours.

2.5 Comprehensive Auditing & Remediation Framework

Every franchise location is subject to systematic clinical audits evaluated by HQ across three performance tiers:

AUDIT TIER & REMEDIATION MATRIX [Tier 1: Full Compliance] --------> Excellence Award & Franchise Renewal Eligibility [Tier 2: Minor Non-Compliance] ----> CAPA Plan (30-Day Resolution Window) [Tier 3: Critical Violation] -----> Operational Cure Period / Suspension / Termination
Schedule & Methodology: Bi-Annual Clinical Audits (comprehensive on-site/digital review of medical records, compounding logs, cold-chain maintenance) + Unannounced Spot Audits triggered automatically upon logging an SAE or client complaint.

Detailed Audit Domains, Protocols & Outcomes

Domain A: Medical Records, Consent & Tier Triage
Protocol Requirement: 100% of client charts must contain fully executed, signed Informed Consent Forms corresponding to the exact treatment tier administered.

Outcome Achieved (Score ≥95%) Full operational compliance verified. Facility receives HQ Certification of Clinical Excellence.

Minor Deficit (Score 80%−94%) Missing signatures on secondary documentation, delayed chart closure (>24 hours post-treatment). HQ issues Notice of Minor Deficit; CAPA required within 30 days.

Critical Breach (Score <80%) Administering Tier 2 or Tier 3 therapies without executed consent or unapproved protocols. Immediate Notice of Critical Breach, clinical hold on non-compliant protocols, and $15,000 penalty fee.

Domain B: Compounding, Storage & Cold-Chain Integrity
Protocol Requirement: Strict adherence to 503A/503B compounding standards, continuous digital temperature monitoring for all biologicals (2°C−8°C), and 100% traceability of lot/batch numbers.

Outcome Achieved Zero temperature excursions; 100% complete batch tracking logs. Full authorization to continue compounding.

Minor Deficit Manual temperature logging gap (<24 hours) with no compromise to biological integrity, or minor labeling deficiencies. CAPA issued; mandatory installation of automated IoT telemetry within 14 days.

Critical Breach Cold-chain failure (>4 hours excursion outside safe ranges) without inventory destruction, or unverified peptide suppliers. Mandatory destruction of affected inventory at Franchisee expense under HQ supervision; immediate suspension of compounding license.

Domain C: Emergency Readiness & Safety Protocol Execution
Protocol Requirement: Monthly crash cart inspections, functional AED certification, valid staff Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) certifications, and active hospital Transfer Agreement.

Outcome Achieved Fully operational emergency infrastructure verified; zero safety audit failures.

Minor Deficit Single staff member's ACLS certification expired within past 30 days, or incomplete daily crash cart checklist. Staff member restricted from clinical duties until recertified; CAPA required within 7 days.

Critical Breach Absence of an active tertiary hospital Transfer Agreement, non-functional emergency equipment, or uncertified resuscitation staff. Immediate temporary closure of facility by HQ order until protocols are fully rectified.

2.6 Next-Frontier Legal Protocols & Brand Protection Safeguards

🛡️ Whistleblower Shield

HQ maintains a centralized, encrypted, third-party-hosted portal accessible 24/7. Staff may anonymously report clinical safety bypasses, forced administration of unapproved compounds, or falsified audit logs with guaranteed anti-retaliation legal protection.

🚨 Public Crisis & Step-In Rights

Triggered by patient complications, media crisis, regulatory raids, or severe adverse events. HQ reserves the absolute right to issue an Immediate 48-Hour Operational Freeze and assume sole control over communications, filings, and clinical investigations.

📋 Continuity of Care Protocol

In the event of franchise suspension or termination, patient care must never be compromised. The franchisee contractually agrees to immediately transfer patient records, diagnostic tracking, and prepaid escrow funds to HQ or a designated custodianship clinic.
