🏛️ 10.0 Chapter Overview & Financial/Legal Governance

Chapter 10 establishes the commercial, financial, and legal framework governing the deployment and operation of You Wellness Clinic franchises, with specific structural adaptation for the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region.

Financial sustainability and clinical compliance are interdependent. Operating a high-touch precision longevity facility requires balancing capital expenditure (CapEx), operational costs (OpEx), recurring revenue streams, and regulatory compliance. This chapter integrates the membership pricing structures from Chapter 8 into a multi-year financial model, defines the franchisor fee engine, establishes corporate practice of medicine (CPOM) isolation frameworks across key MENA jurisdictions (United Arab Emirates, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and Egypt), and outlines franchisor Step-In Rights to protect network equity.

💳 4. Royalties & Tech Licenses (10% of Revenue)

Network royalties, tech infrastructure fees, Spike API integration, and central marketing contributions.

10.3 Franchise Fee Structure, Royalty Engine & Billing Mechanics

FRANCHISOR FEE STRUCTURE & ROYALTY ENGINE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── 1. INITIAL FRANCHISE FEE: Fixed upfront payment upon license agreement execution. • Flagship: $150,000 | Urban Outpatient: $100,000 | Resort Shop-in-Shop: $75,000 2. CONTINUING ROYALTY FEE (6.0% of Gross Revenue): • Assessed monthly against all membership dues, individual therapy fees, and diagnostic charges. 3. DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE & SPIKE API FEE (2.0% of Gross Revenue): • Direct funding for Spike API maintenance, zero-trust cloud servers, EHR software updates, and bi-weekly AI diagnostic algorithm tuning. 4. GLOBAL BRAND & MARKETING FUND (2.0% of Gross Revenue): • Centralized brand assets, HNWI acquisition campaigns, global PR, and brand protection.
💳 10.3.1 Automated Clearinghouse & EHR Payment Interlock

1. Automated Remittance: Fees are automatically calculated and debited on the 5th business day of each calendar month via integrated payment gateways (e.g., Stripe Connect, Network International, or local MENA banking rails).

2. Non-Payment Penalty Interlock: If royalty or software fees remain unpaid past 15 calendar days:

  • Interest Assessment: Default interest accrues at 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or maximum legal rate.
  • EHR Soft Lock (Day 20): Access to non-essential platform upgrades and central marketing support is suspended.
  • EHR Hard Lock (Day 30): Central EHR system freezes active scheduling functions for new members until accounts are cleared.

⚖️ 10.4.1 Corporate Practice of Medicine (CPOM) Structure

In jurisdictions restricting direct corporate ownership of medical licenses (e.g., Egypt, select KSA structures), franchisees must execute a dual-entity MedCo / OpCo legal structure:

FRANCHISE HOLDING COMPANY (OpCo) • Owns physical real estate & equipment. • Employs admin & hospitality personnel. • Receives commercial membership fees. │ ▼ (MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT & IP LICENSE) │ PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL CORPORATION (MedCo) • 100% Owned by Licensed Local Medical Practitioner(s). • Employs Physicians, Pharmacists, and RNs. • Holds direct DHA / MoH / MoHP facility licenses.
🌐 10.4.2 Cross-Border Data Sovereignty Compliance

To satisfy UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, Saudi NDMO regulations, and Egyptian Law 151 of 2020:

  • All patient health information (PHI), biometric data, diagnostic reports, and EHR logs originating in a MENA country must be stored on primary local cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS UAE/KSA regions or local tier-4 data centers).
  • Cross-border transmission via the Spike API is limited to zero-knowledge encrypted, de-identified telemetry payloads used for central diagnostic algorithm computation.

🚨 10.5.2 Franchisor Step-In Rights & Operational Cure Mechanics
Intervention Triggers
  • 1. Audit Failure: Tier C score (<700 Points) on quarterly audit.
  • 2. Severe Clinical Breach: Unremediated SAE or intentional dosing cap override resulting in patient harm.
  • 3. Regulatory Suspension: License suspension or revocation by DHA / MoH / MoHP.
  • 4. Financial Default: Non-remittance of monthly royalties for >45 calendar days.
Step-In Execution Sequence

Phase I: Notice & Suspension (Day 0)
Formal Notice of Deficiency issued; EHR locks new member scheduling and Tier 3 biologicals.

Phase II: Management Take-Over (Days 1–60)
Interim Medical Director & Ops Lead deployed; Franchisee pays $2,500/day management fees plus expenses.

Phase III: Cure or Termination (Day 60)
Option A: Cure achieved (Tier A score ≥850), control returned.
Option B: Termination & OpCo asset buyout at 30% discount to FMV.

⚖️ 10.6 Dispute Resolution, Governing Law & Jurisdiction

1. Governing Law

Governed by substantive laws of DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre) or ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market) for regional contracts.

2. Arbitration Mechanism

Binding arbitration under rules of DIAC (Dubai) or SCCA (Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration).

3. Emergency Injunctions

Right reserved to seek immediate local court injunctive relief for IP, patient safety, or non-compete violations.
