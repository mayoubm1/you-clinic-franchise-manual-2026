6.1.1 Mandatory Consent Protocols & Governance Rules

📋 Form A
General Wellness & Diagnostic Agreement

Executed during initial member onboarding. Covers non-invasive diagnostic panels, continuous bio-telemetry, baseline DEXA scans, and standard oral supplementation.

🧪 Form B
Compounded & Off-Label Precision Consent

Required prior to initiating any Tier 2 compounded intravenous or subcutaneous peptide therapy. Explicitly outlines off-label precision usage, compounded pharmaceutical origin (FDA 503A/503B or local equivalent), potential metabolic side effects, and required diagnostic monitoring schedules.

🧬 Form C
Advanced Regenerative & Ethics Waiver

Mandatory for all Tier 3 cell-derived or exosome protocols. Details biological origin, batch traceability, potential immunogenic risks, and requires explicit acknowledgment of institutional/ethics board review parameters.

🔒 6.4.1 EHR Interlock Mechanism

When a clinician selects a controlled formulation or therapy, the EHR system scans the member's central health record, active biotelemetry streams, and lab results. If any exclusion parameter is triggered, the system executes an immediate prescribing lock.

🔑 6.4.2 Dual Digital Authorization Override

Overriding a soft contraindication flag requires Dual Digital Authorization:

  • 1. Cryptographic digital sign-off from Local Medical Director + written justification.
  • 2. Cryptographic digital sign-off from HQ Clinical Safety Officer.
NON-OVERRIDEABLE: Absolute hard stops (such as active cancer with growth peptides or G6PD deficiency with high-dose Vitamin C) cannot be overridden under any circumstances.

6.5 Medical Device SOPs: HBOT, Cryotherapy & Photobiomodulation

🫧 6.5.1 HBOT Protocols
  • Clearance: FDA 510(k) or EU-MDR Class IIb; ASME PVHO-1 compliance.
  • Dosing: 1.5 ATA – 2.0 ATA for 60 to 90 mins (100% O₂). Air breaks every 30 mins; ascent/descent ≤ 0.1 bar/min.
  • Screening: Otoscopic tympanic membrane eval required. Exclude active sinus congestion, pneumothorax history, or bullous lung disease.
  • Operator: Certified Hyperbaric Technologist (CHT) or hyperbaric certified RN.
❄️ 6.5.2 Whole-Body Cryotherapy
  • Safety Clearance: CE medical mark or FDA registration. Continuous O₂ sensors with auto cut-off if room O₂ < 19.5%.
  • Operational Limits: Max 180 seconds (3.0 mins) at −110 °C to −140 °C.
  • Exclusions: BP ≥ 140/90, Raynaud's, cold urticaria, unstable cardiac conditions.
  • Gear: Protective footwear, thermal gloves, ear protection, dry attire mandatory.
🔴 6.5.3 Photobiomodulation (PBM)
  • Wavelengths: Dual red (660 nm) and near-infrared (850 nm) LED arrays.
  • Power Density: 20–50 mW/cm² delivering cumulative energy fluence of 10–30 J/cm² per session.
  • Eye Protection: Mandatory OD 5+ rated protective eyewear targeting 600–900 nm spectrums for members and operators.
