🏛️ 9.0 Chapter Overview & Human Capital Governance

Human capital is the leverage point of the entire franchise standard. Clinical trust, luxury hospitality, and regulatory defensibility are only as strong as the credentialed individuals delivering them. Chapter 9 consolidates the mandatory staffing ratios, professional credentialing rules, automated EHR license interlocks, and the You Academy certification curriculum that every franchise facility must operate before and throughout active clinical service.

All staffing ratios are designed to satisfy global labor-law mandates and regional nurse-to-patient requirements while preserving the white-glove service ratios defined in Chapter 4 (Brand Identity & Luxury Patient Experience). Credentialing enforcement is automatic: no license, no login, no clinical authority — enforced by the EHR credential interlock established in Chapter 3 (Health Data Governance & Cybersecurity).

9.1 Staffing Credentials, Ratios & Medical Licensing Requirements

Clinical quality relies on mandatory staff credentialing and strict practitioner-to-patient operational ratios across all 5 spatial zones.

⚖️ 9.1.1 Global Labor Law & Staffing Ratios Governance

All facilities must comply with local labor laws and regional nurse-to-patient ratio mandates (e.g., California CDPH, UK Royal College of Nursing, UAE DHA/MOHAP regulations). Where local statutory mandates require lower patient-to-nurse ratios than HQ guidelines, the more stringent requirement shall apply.

🫀 9.1.2 Mandatory ACLS Credentialing Standard

All Registered Nurses (RNs), Nurse Practitioners (NPs), Physician Assistants (PAs), Hyperbaric Technologists (CHTs), and Physicians operating on the clinical floor must maintain active Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) certification issued by the American Heart Association (AHA) or an equivalent international body. Basic Life Support (BLS) alone is insufficient for clinical floor authorization.

🔒 9.1.3 License Verification & Automated EHR Credential Interlock

Professional licenses, ACLS certifications, liability insurance policies, and specialty accreditations must be uploaded to the HQ Human Capital EHR portal prior to employment. The EHR tracks credential expiration dates and issues automated warnings:

60-Day Warning

Email notification sent to staff member and Local Medical Director.

30-Day Warning

Flagged alert displayed upon daily EHR login.

7-Day Final Warning

Mandatory administrative notification sent to HQ Compliance Officer.

Hard Lockout Enforcement: At 00:01 on the date of credential expiration, the EHR automatically revokes login credentials, clinical ordering capabilities, and access to patient files. Operational activity by an uncredentialed staff member constitutes a Major Compliance Breach subject to an immediate $10,000 fine.

9.2.2 Mandatory Quarterly Simulation Drills

Every facility must conduct and document four emergency simulation drills per year. Drills must be video-recorded, logged in the QMS portal, and audited by HQ.

🚨 Drill Alpha: Acute Medical Crisis

Scenario: Acute severe anaphylactic reaction or vasovagal syncope during Tier 2/3 infusion.

Required Response: Immediate cessation of infusion, Code Blue activation, Trendelenburg position placement, therapy initiation, protocol-driven epinephrine/diphenhydramine administration from crash cart, and EMS contact within 60 seconds.

⚡ Drill Bravo: Device Emergency & Barotrauma / Cryo Cut-Off

Scenario: Pressure loss/ear barotrauma in HBOT chamber, or ambient O₂ < 19.5% in Cryo suite.

Required Response: Manual emergency depressurization/ventilation protocols, immediate patient evacuation, neurological & otoscopic evaluation, and secondary environmental exhaust activation.

❄️ Drill Charlie: Facility Infrastructure & Cold-Chain Failure

Scenario: Total mains power outage combined with simulated UPS failover during peak compounding.

Required Response: Automated ATS generator start verification within 8 seconds, manual inspection of ULT freezer power inputs, Spike API offline buffer confirmation, and cleanroom emergency gown-out SOP.

💻 Drill Delta: EHR Downtime & Cyber Incident

Scenario: Simulated loss of cloud WAN connectivity during active clinical operations.

Required Response: Conversion to local zero-knowledge encrypted edge-node operating mode, manual verification of dosing caps using physical laminated reference charts, and post-restoration data synchronization.

🔗 9.3 HR Continuity & Franchise Governance Link
  • Credentials Surveillance: Continuous clinical-credentials surveillance and expiry enforcement (60 / 30 / 7-day warnings plus hard lockout) are executed by the HQ Human Capital EHR portal defined in Chapter 3, with credential status audited within Domain D of the Chapter 7 1,000-point master audit scorecard.
  • QMS Accountability: Staff disciplinary, pharmacovigilance, and adverse-event accountability flows are governed by Chapter 7 Quality Management System (QMS) protocols, ensuring HR actions remain clinically and legally documented.
  • Financial Integration: Recruitment ratios and payroll structures feed the Chapter 10 financial model (Clinical & Administrative Payroll: 30–35% of revenue), keeping unit economics consistent with the published pro forma.
